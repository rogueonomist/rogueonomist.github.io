Situation: The existing COI (Certificate of Investment) at Pak Oman, PKR 1,300mn at 10.05%, matures on 31 July 2026. The Pak Oman credit facility (PKR 1,238mn, rate 11.71% from ledger) is the linked borrowing. The rollover of both legs — a new COI placement at whatever rate Pak Oman offers — is the most operationally natural path.

Carry improvement on rollover: Current COI (10.05%) is below market. New COI placement rate will be at prevailing market — likely 10.50–11.50% based on current T-Bill auction yields (3M: 11.51%, 12M: 11.99%). At even the conservative end (10.50%): post-tax carry improves from +PKR 16.0mn p.a. to +PKR 20.2mn p.a. At 11.00%: +PKR 26.0mn p.a. This is a material improvement.

IPS constraint: The IPS §3.2(d) flag is ACTIVE — COI represents ~59% of the non-sovereign/non-bank FI sleeve vs. 20% limit. Rolling extends this breach. Board/IC interpretation is required before rollover is approved. This flag does not block rollover — it blocks approval without Board/IC sign-off.

MPC interaction: If MPC holds today (97% probability), KIBOR stays at current levels → facility cost unchanged until Sep-29 reset. The rollover economics are not materially affected by today's decision. If MPC cuts (3%), KIBOR drops → facility cost falls at Sep-29 reset → carry improves further, but the new COI rate would also be bid lower. Net effect: still positive carry.

Recommended action: (1) Contact Pak Oman today to confirm COI rollover rate (do not wait for MPC). (2) If Pak Oman offers ≥10.50% — APPROVE ROLLOVER subject to Board/IC IPS interpretation. (3) If Board/IC has not resolved the §3.2(d) flag — CEO must decide to proceed or exit on an interim basis. (4) An alternative is partial redemption of COI (PKR 300–500mn) to reduce concentration — discuss with Pak Oman as they may require full utilisation of facility.

Decision Required (CEO): Approve COI/Pak Oman rollover at prevailing rate, subject to IPS §3.2(d) Board/IC interpretation gate — or direct alternative action by 31 July 2026.
