What's In the HR Manual

Part D (Compensation), Part E(i) (Performance Management), and Part E(ii) (Quarterly Performance & Bonus Framework) — this dashboard mirrors all three sections as currently proposed.

What Changed From the Original

One evaluation scale (O/S/M/U with a 4-point rating alongside) replaces the old dual scale; the Score Range column has been removed from both Band tables; the bonus pool now runs on a single formula; multipliers are fixed values; and the approval chain is one explicit sentence.

Part E(i) & E(ii) · Process

Two Operating Cadences Feed One Year-End Rating

An annual cycle sets direction and confirms the final rating; a 13-week quarterly rhythm does the actual measuring, using five scorecard categories and a RACI credit-sharing model for cross-functional work.

Annual Performance Management Cycle

1
Goal Setting
Post-probation · January/February
Employees set goals with their supervisor, including deadlines, weightages, and specified outcomes — approved by the HoD and sent to HR.
2
Interim Review
Mid-year
Progress against goals is reviewed; changes can be made in consultation with the performance manager. Cross-functional contributions are documented here.
3
Year-End Review
December
Self-assessment plus supervisor ranking; the CEO reviews and approves all ratings. Annual increments take effect from 1 July.

The Quarterly Operating Rhythm — 13 Weeks, Four Times a Year

1
Quarterly Planning
Weeks -2 to 0
Leadership sets 3–5 Company Objectives; each department proposes 2–4 Team Objectives with specific Key Results. Capacity-checked and approved by the CEO and Management Committee.
2
Execution & Monitoring
Weeks 1–12
Weekly squad syncs (30–45 min) on progress and blockers; bi-weekly check-ins tracking metrics against Key Results; one formal mid-quarter re-baselining window (weeks 6–7) under change-control.
3
Quarter Close & Review
Week 13
Teams submit final evidence; scorecards are locked. Finance calculates Adjusted PAT and the preliminary bonus pool. The year-end cycle adds cumulative annual assessment, compensation decisions, and promotion reviews.

What Stayed the Same

  • The Board still sets every pool input — hurdle, X%, Y%, the cost/income ceiling, and any reserve/extraordinary-item adjustments.
  • The risk gate and final-scaling safeguards are unchanged.
  • The quarterly scoring scale (0.0–1.2 per Key Result) and the five scorecard categories are unchanged — only the Band table's presentation was simplified.
