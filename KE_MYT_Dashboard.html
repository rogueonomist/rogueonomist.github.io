00 · Overview

Executive Summary

KE's petition asks NEPRA to lock in a recovery-loss and distribution-loss allowance that blends a shrinking pool of genuinely hard-to-collect areas with a much larger pool of ordinary, collectable, middle-and-upper-slab consumption. Every finding below is sourced and cross-checked — filter by evidence class to see what's documented (A), recomputed (B), or a cross-file finding (C).

What this means for the negotiating position. None of these findings require disputing K-Electric's right to recover prudent costs. They support a narrower, more defensible ask: tie any recovery-loss or distribution-loss allowance to feeder/IBC-level evidence rather than a company-wide average — because the company-wide average is being pulled down by a well-identified, quantifiable pool of controllable underperformance that sits largely outside the "law and order" justification KE offers for it.

Class C — Anomaly #1

The petition's own headline figure does not reconcile. Slide 22 states both "PKR 176.8 bn cumulative savings" and "~PKR 30 bn per year." Seven years at PKR 30bn is ≈PKR 210bn, not 176.8bn. Independently re-summing the same slide's own year-by-year table — cross-checked against the underlying workbook — gives PKR 209.8 bn, which reconciles with the "~30bn/year" claim but not the "176.8bn" headline. The 176.8bn figure could not be reproduced from any combination of the underlying data and should be treated as unverified until KE clarifies its derivation.

Basis caution: savings are estimated against a PKR 750bn revenue-requirement base. YTD-FY25 revenue is already trending above PKR 950bn — both savings streams (and the cost of failing to secure them) will scale up proportionally on the current base.

12 · Negotiation Toolkit

Data Gaps & Further Disclosure Requests

The evidence base is unusually rich for a DISCO tariff negotiation, but closing these specific gaps would materially strengthen the ability to hold KE to feeder/IBC-level accountability.

Feeder→IBC mapping. No published link between the 455 HLF and the 30 IBCs, preventing an exact tie-out.
Reconcile the two "Recommended T&D" trajectories. The workbook and the final deck disagree — confirm the authoritative version in writing.
Derivation of PKR 176.8bn. Could not be reproduced from any combination of underlying data reviewed.
Feeder-level time series. 36 months of sent-out/billed/collected data at full feeder granularity.
Default ageing by IBC. Would show whether large defaulters cluster in already-identified high-loss areas.
Explicit tariff/value basis. Files imply different PKR/kWh values (≈Rs 59 vs. ≈Rs 42) for monetized claims.
AMI/meter-replacement deployment. To test whether "bad connections" losses are being remediated MYT to MYT.
Updated TDS savings on current revenue base. PKR 750bn base vs. YTD-FY25 trending above PKR 950bn.
