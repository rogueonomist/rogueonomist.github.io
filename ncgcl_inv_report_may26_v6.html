Fixed Income Market Review & Portfolio Activity
Market Review — May 2026

Treasury Bills. The PKRV short end repriced sharply upward: the 6M rose 50 bps to 12.37%; 9M and 12M increased 51 and 48 bps; 3M moved +25 bps to 11.94%. Tighter liquidity and elevated-for-longer rate expectations drove the adjustment. The 1M cut-off increased 86 bps to 12.23% at the 20 May auction.

PIBs. The long end compressed: the 5Y fell 48 bps to 12.37%; 10Y and 20Y declined 38 and 30 bps. Curve flattening reflects differentiated views on near-term funding costs versus medium-term inflation. PIB bids were rejected in both recent auctions.

Portfolio Strategy & Forward Outlook

Current positioning. NCGCL's decisive reallocation from underperforming mutual funds into Market Treasury Bills in May 2026 has materially strengthened the portfolio's income profile. MTBs now represent 75% of assets, diversified across 3-, 6-, and 12-month tenors — securing sovereign yields at or above benchmark levels with full liquidity. The corporate Sukuk book (Daewoo Express at 13.09%; At-Tahur/PREMA at 3MK+130 bps; both A1-rated) adds a targeted 60–100 bps spread premium without materially extending duration or credit risk.

Near-term priorities. The team will sustain active primary market participation, pursuing MTB allocations across the 3M–6M tenor spectrum where risk-adjusted yields remain compelling. As the Daewoo Express Sukuk matures in August 2026, proceeds will be redeployed at prevailing market rates, providing an additional yield uplift opportunity. Duration will remain conservative; any extension will be conditional on a credible, data-backed inflection in the policy rate trajectory.

Outlook. The YTD yield gap of −20 bps against the benchmark is a direct artefact of the portfolio's Jan–Apr composition; the May realignment to 11.13% demonstrates that this gap is structural no longer. With a 9th-of-43 peer ranking, PKR 293mn in YTD income, and a 0.47% asset base expansion, NCGCL's investment portfolio is well-positioned to sustain above-benchmark returns and defend its peer ranking through the remainder of 2026. Capital preservation, liquidity adequacy, and disciplined yield optimisation remain the governing principles.
