How the two paths are built

Buy
You pay the price at Year 0 and occupy the property. Purchase costs are paid at Year 0, and each year you pay property tax, service charges, maintenance and insurance from outside the portfolio. That cash could have earned a return, so every outflow is compounded at the opportunity rate (default: the net fixed income yield) and deducted from the property's value.
Invest & rent
The same amount goes into the portfolio. Fixed income earns its return on the opening balance, withholding tax comes off the profit, and the rent is paid from what is left. A surplus is reinvested; a shortfall is drawn from fixed income principal first, then equity. Equity compounds without tax until it is sold.
Hold or sell everything
Hold compares mark-to-market values, as in your brief: no tax on unrealised equity gains and no selling costs on the property. Sell everything deducts selling costs and CGT on the property gain, and CGT on the equity gain.
Timing
Annual steps. Income accrues on opening balances and payments fall at year end, so monthly rent is treated as one annual payment.

Left out

  • Tax relief on rent or property costs if the premises are used in a business, and depreciation.
  • Borrowing. Both paths use the full amount in cash.
  • Equity dividends, rebalancing, and fund or brokerage charges on the portfolio.
  • Adjustability of the advance taxes on purchase and sale: the purchase tax is counted as a cost, the sale-side advance tax is ignored in favour of CGT.
  • Vacancy, fit-out and relocation costs, and any difference in how a landlord and a tenant would treat the premises.

Tax and cost inputs

Rates below come from secondary summaries of the Finance Act 2026, last checked October 2026. Confirm them with your tax adviser before relying on them.

  • The deemed-income tax on property (Section 7E) was struck down in May 2026 and omitted from 1 July 2026, so it is not modelled. ICT summary.
  • Advance tax on purchase (Section 236K) is a flat 1.25% for filers from 1 July 2026. ICT rate guide.
  • Capital gains tax is a flat 15% on property acquired after 1 July 2024 and on listed shares for filers. Baco Consultants.
  • The CBC property tax is assessed on an FBR-notified annual rental value and has been revised steeply, with some notices up 100% or more. I could not find a published rate schedule, so the property tax default is a placeholder: use "Fixed PKR per year" with your actual bill. ProPakistani.
  • Stamp duty, registration, CVT, service charges, maintenance and insurance are placeholders. Replace them with quotes.
