How Much Pakistan Spends on Eid-ul-Azha:
Animals, Meat, Hides, Logistics & Incidentals

This dashboard converts three-day municipal offal collections, Pakistan Tanners Association hide/skin counts, and a new macro cash-cycle method based on Currency in Circulation around Eid into a transparent estimate of sacrificial animals, edible meat, hide value and total spending from 2022 to 2025. The national headline uses PTA hide/skin counts; city estimates use a weighted offal-to-head framework. Incidentals are capped below the user-specified ceiling of 15% of animal cost.

Base case: holding period 3 days · incidentals 12% of animal cost · offal kg/head by species: cattle 70, buffalo 80, goat/sheep 5, camel 120 · meat yield: cattle 140 kg, buffalo 150 kg, goat/sheep 12 kg, camel 250 kg · prices benchmarked to NGO/share-price envelopes · cash-cycle method uses SBP Currency in Circulation and M2 around Eid.

Exhibit 5 — Macro Cash-Cycle Method: Broad Money, Currency in Circulation & Eid Spending
Why this method matters
Eid-ul-Azha is cash intensive because livestock markets, transporters, fodder sellers, butchers and small traders still operate heavily in cash. The model therefore uses weekly SBP monetary data to isolate the abnormal increase in Currency in Circulation (CIC) before Eid, controls for normal rolling-seasonal movements, and converts the abnormal cash pulse into a spending-equivalent estimate. This provides an independent macro cross-check against the animal-count and offal models.
Transaction-equivalent multiplier:Interpretation:CIC is a stock; Eid spending is a flow. Multiplier converts abnormal cash stock into transaction-equivalent flow.

Empirical anchors

Religious feast effectCash demand rises pre-Eid, reverses after

Return lag2–4 weeks in practice
Main caveatCIC stock ≠ total transactions
Methodology note: Weekly observations are used because the SBP Broad Money archive reports CIC and M2 at weekly dates. The rolling baseline controls for ordinary week-to-week cash variation, month-end effects, wage cycles and monetary trend. The method is deliberately conservative: it captures the incremental cash stock outside the banking system that is statistically unusual during Eid, not the full value of all animal purchases, digital transfers or cash already circulating outside banks.

Exhibit 8 — Source Register and Data Gaps
Evidence discipline
Every offal and hide data point is tagged with a source, confidence level and measurement note. High confidence indicates actual three-day operations from municipal or official/credible reporting. Medium confidence indicates mixed scope, conflicting definitions or division-level scope. Low confidence indicates plans, estimates, first-day-only figures, or unclear provenance.

Important caveat: The dashboard distinguishes between national PTA hide/skin counts and municipal offal-derived estimates. The PTA model is the preferred estimate for national spending. Municipal rows are not a national total; they are a coverage sample and city-level cross-check. All figures are model estimates and should be refreshed when official municipal completion reports or PTA annual data are released.
