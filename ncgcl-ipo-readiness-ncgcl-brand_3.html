GoP-Managed Portfolio Build (PKR B) — The IPO Swing Factor

Investment income and guarantee fees alone yield a Jun-29 ROE of approximately 8–9%. The GoP management fee — earned at 0.5% on guarantees managed on behalf of the Government of Pakistan — closes the gap to 18%. The model requires a GoP-managed portfolio approaching PKR 400B by Jun-29 to achieve listing eligibility at 2× Book.
