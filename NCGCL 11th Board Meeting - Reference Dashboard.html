2b

Amendment to the Finance & Administration Manual

Decision

The current Manual is silent on intangible assets. The proposed Section 7b — Intangible Assets Accounting Guidelines closes this gap for software products the Company controls and expects to benefit from for more than one year.

  • Recognition and measurement aligned to IAS 38; carried at cost less accumulated amortization and impairment.
  • Amortization: straight-line at 25% per year, from the date the software is ready for use until derecognition.
  • Intangibles with indefinite useful lives are excluded from amortization.
Decision required: Approve the addition of Section 7b to the Finance & Administration Manual.

Facility-by-Facility Detail
3a

HBL Zarai Services Ltd — PKR 37.5M

Policy Deviation

Overview: A controlled, evidence-first pilot of Zarai's deferred-settlement / receivables financing model across four Dera hubs in Punjab (~1,500 acres, 2026 Kharif & Rabi seasons). NCGCL absorbs 25% of a PKR 150M receivables portfolio through a two-layer waterfall — pari passu (11–30% loss band, PKR 15M) and a tail layer (31–45% loss band, PKR 22.5M).

Risk mitigants: mandatory parametric crop insurance, harvest mechanization via Zarai's machinery pool, weekly GPS-tagged field visits, anti-collusion field-team rotation, and unannounced NCGCL spot-checks (minimum 25 growers).

Policy deviations flagged: Zarai is not a regulated/licensed financial institution under SBP or SECP; its lending track record falls short of the 3-year threshold; and it is unrated by a local credit rating agency (Guarantee Policy 2025 §5.1(a), §5.2(a)/(e), §9.2).
Decision required: Approve the PKR 37.5M pilot facility and waive the above policy provisions for this one-season sandbox deployment, ahead of an end-of-season review before any Rabi 2026 continuation.
3b

JS Bank Limited — PKR 2,000M

Compliant

Pak China Investment Co., Saudi Pak Industrial & Agricultural Investment Co., PMRC, ASA Microfinance Bank, RCDP, Kashf Foundation, JWS Pakistan, DAMEN, Agahe Pakistan), with guarantee cover tiered 20%/30%/40% by counterparty.

Decision required: Approve the PKR 2,000M portfolio pari passu guarantee facility; no policy deviations observed.

Compliant

Institution: Bank Alfalah — AAA/A1+, Abu Dhabi Group-backed (56.06% sponsor holding); total assets PKR 3,038B; CAR 16.22% (Mar-2026); 1,179 branches nationwide.

3d

HBL Microfinance Bank — PKR 2,500M

Policy Deviation

Overview: Account-level pari passu guarantee (up to 50% loss coverage) on new/enhanced lending to crop farmers, livestock borrowers, and microenterprises — split PKR 1,000M (Crop), PKR 1,000M (Livestock), PKR 500M (Microenterprise).

Institution: HBL MFB — A+/A1 (Positive), anchored by AAA/A1+ parent HBL within the Aga Khan Development Network; returned to profit in CY25 (PBT PKR 1,600M vs prior-year PKR 5,900M loss); CAR comfortably above regulatory floor; conservative provisioning (~142% of NPLs).

Policy deviation flagged: Guarantee Policy 2025 caps single-obligor exposure for an A+-rated entity at PKR 1,260M; the proposed PKR 2,500M exceeds this threshold by roughly PKR 1,240M.
Decision required: Approve the PKR 2,500M facility and explicitly waive the single-obligor concentration cap under Guarantee Policy 2025 given HBL MFB's credit standing and parent-backed rating.

4a

Board Evaluation Framework

Decision

Overview: A formal Board Evaluation Framework, developed against the 4Q24 Due Diligence report's recommendations, assessed across four lenses: the whole Board (strategy, risk, culture), Board committees (TOR fit and output quality), individual directors (preparation, independence, ethics), and the Chairperson (leadership).

Decision required: Approve the Board Evaluation Framework and the accompanying NCGCL Board Self-Evaluation Questionnaire.
4b

Amendments to the NCGCL HR Manual (incl. Employee Vehicle Policy)

Decision

Overview: The April 2026 HR Manual (Master Consolidated Edition, v2.0) is a substantive rewrite of the October 2025 Manual — not a light edit — repositioning it from a process-heavy handbook into a governance document for a lean, matrix-structured institution.

  • Governance front matter: document control, effective date, review cycle, and a clear policy hierarchy.
  • Matrix orientation: a new "Manager Fundamentals" section — 1:1s, goal clarity, early feedback, structured hiring, inclusive leadership.
  • Performance & bonus architecture: a 13-week RACI-based quarterly operating cycle layered onto the annual PMS.
  • Formalization: internships, remote work, and professional-examination sponsorship are now codified.
  • Employee vehicle policy: Car Monetization Allowance provisions are retained within Part D (Compensation & Benefits) of the redraft.
Management and the Committee have confirmed that no compensation limits, benefit amounts, or monetary entitlements previously approved by the Board have been changed — this is a restructuring and clarity exercise, not a compensation change.
Decision required: Approve the April 2026 HR Manual as the Master Consolidated Edition, superseding the October 2025 version.

Agenda 9 · 1505–1510 · Any Other Item, With Permission of the Chair

SME Export Structured Finance & Guarantee Programme

Raised under Any Other Item — this paper is not one of the nine numbered items on the printed notice, but is marked "For Decision" in the working paper itself and is included here for the Board's awareness ahead of the meeting.

Overview: The Federal Budget FY2026-27 has allocated a PKR 1 billion non-returnable grant to NCGCL for the promotion of export-oriented SMEs. The proposed Export-Oriented SME Structuring & Guarantee Programme deploys this as catalytic risk capital across nine chartered product windows (trade finance, productivity enhancement, working capital), delivered through Partner Financial Institutions and EXIM Bank in a bundled model.

Grant (Risk Capital)
PKR 1B

PKR 2–4B

Incremental Annual Exports

The market case targets 1,471 addressable export SMEs across 43 HS chapters (TDAP exporter registers, FY2020-21 basis). The paper argues Pakistan's binding constraint is not loanable funds but a structuring/bankability gap at the "discovery margin" — new products, buyers, or markets — which existing concessional schemes (EFS, LTFF) do not reach. Deployment is proposed over a phased 24-month roadmap with a Phase 4 evidence gate before scale-up.

Decision required (pending Chair's permission to table under AOB): Approve the framework for deployment of the PKR 1B grant as catalytic risk capital under the Programme.
