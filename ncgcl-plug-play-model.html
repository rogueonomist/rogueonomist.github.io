Exhibit 5 · Shareholder Proposition

Why capital routed through NCGCL creates more impact

NCGCL can continuously crowd in capital from multilaterals, federal and provincial governments, sponsors and other entities—expanding both the scale and thematic reach of guarantees.

Every PKR enabled through NCGCL can support PKR 8.57 of financing—while preserving programme control, ring-fencing and measurable impact.

The platform is especially powerful where targeted borrowers are commercially viable but constrained by lender risk appetite, collateral gaps, first-loss concerns or new-technology risk.

Plug & play deployment

Capital providers use NCGCL’s existing regulated infrastructure rather than building a new delivery institution.

Programme ring-fencing

Capital can be segregated by provider, sector, geography, target group, product and loss waterfall.

Revolving impact

Subject to losses and programme terms, released guarantee capacity can be redeployed into new eligible financing.

Sponsor liquidity multiplier

The guarantee catalyses liquidity already held by banks and sponsors rather than requiring the state to fund every loan.

Interpretation note: The PKR 103.73Bn figure is incremental underlying financing relative to a simple 1:1 deployment of PKR 13.7Bn. It should not be described as regulatory capital released. Actual bank capital relief depends on guarantee recognition, counterparty substitution, risk weights, IFRS 9 treatment, portfolio composition and applicable SBP rules. The 3× leverage and 35% cover are portfolio-level planning assumptions; individual programmes—especially first-loss structures—may support different multipliers.
