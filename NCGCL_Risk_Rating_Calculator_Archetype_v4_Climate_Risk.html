Embedded methodology note

Why archetype-based scorecards: a guarantee on a named SME, a portfolio guarantee and an on-lending facility do not create the same risk. In an individual exposure, the final obligor drives default risk. In a programme guarantee, pool quality, concentration and PFI servicing quality dominate. In on-lending, the MFI/NBFC is the borrower/obligor, so its solvency, liquidity, ALM, governance and collections must be underwritten as borrower risk rather than being treated as a minor PFI channel score.

The official calculation is Composite Raw Score = Σ(module score × module weight). Weights must total 100%. The composite IRR is the rounded composite raw score, subject to hard gates, floors, caps, climate ceilings, LGD add-ons and documented override rules.
