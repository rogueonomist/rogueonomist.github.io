Strategic Finding
Pakistan has accumulated ~49 GW of solar modules and ~10 GWh of batteries — without a policy framework to coordinate them. The wholesale price-suppression case for VPPs saturates sharply above 5–6 GW; the argument for an 8–12 GW 2030 target must therefore rest on fuel-import displacement (~PKR 93 billion annually in the accelerated scenario) — not repeated invocation of price compression. The Hollowing Index moved from negative (Feb 2024) to +22.8% average (Apr 2026) across all five major DISCOs: a complete reversal of the daily demand shape in two years. Every additional GW of sustained solar imports deepens the Index by ~2.7 percentage points — a durable, structural shift, not a behavioral fluctuation.

Solar → Battery Elasticity
Early-stage solar showed a log-price elasticity of −1.70 to −1.77 (2022–24): a 10% price decline drove ~17–19% import growth. The 2025 saturation (only +1.7% growth despite continued price decline) reflects market exhaustion and inventory effects — not the underlying technology adoption curve. Battery demand elasticity is estimated at −1.2 (base) with a solar-stock complementarity factor of 0.45, meaning cumulative solar installations directly stimulate battery demand independently of price.

Recommended Architecture
A DISCO-contracted, aggregator-operated, four-hour peak-flexibility programme. Not initially open wholesale trading by individual households — that requires AMI, market infrastructure, and CTBCM readiness that does not yet exist at DISCO level. The VPP aggregates distributed batteries for H17–H21 dispatch, pays on availability + dispatch, and settles via DISCO billing systems on a 15-minute basis.

Institutional Role Chain
Five-layer accountability structure — asset owner → aggregator → DISCO → system operator → regulator
Asset Owner
→
Customer retains ownership and defined backup reserve. Participates via aggregator contract. Override rights and degradation disclosures guaranteed.
VPP Aggregator
→
Enrollment, device certification, demand forecasting, dispatch commands, settlement, cybersecurity compliance, non-delivery guarantees. Licensed by NEPRA. Must hold performance/settlement security.
DISCO
→
Identifies constrained feeders, procures peak reduction, integrates VPP dispatch with network operations. Pays aggregator. Manages staggered recharge to prevent second-peak formation.
System / Market Operator (ISMO)
→
National system needs coordination, wholesale-level settlement, dispatch priority signals. VPP dispatch aligned with H17–H21 peak concentration verified in 26 months of ISMO data.
NEPRA
→
Aggregator licensing, customer protection standards, metering requirements, tariff rules (buyback, availability payments), cybersecurity obligations, data governance, market design review.
VPP Pilot Sequencing
Phase 1 — Shadow VPP
200–500 assets enrolled. No paid dispatch. Tests connectivity, telemetry, cybersecurity protocols, and aggregator performance tracking. Builds DISCO operational muscle.
Phase 2 — Scheduled Dispatch
5–10 MW contracted for announced evening events. Availability + dispatch payments active. Basic performance penalties introduced. Settlement system validated against 15-minute intervals.
Phase 3 — National Scale
Full 10 MW pilot → 100 MW expansion. Real-time ISMO integration. Dynamic pricing signal. Carbon-premium pilot on ToU peak window. Seasonal auction design for maturity-stage buyback.
